[x] Batching: 8 examples from each source domain (24 total per batch).

3. THE THREE REQUIRED METHODS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[x] Method 1: ERM Baseline
    - Reuse the Source-only ERM checkpoint trained in Task 2. Do not retrain.
[x] Method 2: DAN-DG (Pairwise Source-Domain Alignment)
    - Apply MMD to every pair of observed source domains (Photo/Art, Photo/Cartoon, Art/Cartoon).
    - lambda_DG = 1.0. Same 3 RBF kernels (0.5, 1, 2 * median pairwise squared distance).
    - Note: Standard DAN aligns source vs target; DAN-DG aligns source vs source.
[x] Method 3: SAM (Sharpness-Aware Minimization)
    - rho = 0.05.
    - Standard non-adaptive SAM. Two forward/backward passes per step.
    - Frozen BatchNorm running-statistics policy applies during both SAM passes.
